Research notes and sources

The selection combines iconic terminal-era phosphor colors with widely adopted developer themes; “most popular” has no single historical ranking. Classic display context: Smithsonian Amdek P3 amber monitor, IBM 3270 field attributes.

Palette references: Monokai, Solarized, Dracula, Gruvbox, Nord, Tokyo Night, and Catppuccin.
